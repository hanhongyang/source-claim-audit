# Marketplace payout memo (draft)

AgentAlley pays creators monthly. According to its creator terms updated 5 November 2026, a creator's share is 70% of net revenue, each sale is held for 30 days, and payouts go out on the 10th of the following month once the balance reaches $50. Payouts can be sent by PayPal, Payoneer, or bank transfer.

This is a reliable income stream. Typical sellers earn about $2,030 per month — that is 100 sales at $29, after the platform share.

The same terms say refunds are not allowed, so revenue is booked as soon as a sale clears checkout. We should forecast January income using December's gross sales, with no hold.

Users report that approved listings start selling the same day. Demand is exploding, and we will cover our token costs in the first week.
